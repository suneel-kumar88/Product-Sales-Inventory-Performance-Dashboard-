# Product Sales & Inventory Performance Dashboard

This interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track end-to-end supply chain throughput, material movement, sales revenue, procurement costs, and fulfillment backlogs across hardware product lines.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

![Product Sales & Inventory Performance Dashboard] https://github.com/suneel-kumar88/Product-Sales-Inventory-Performance-Dashboard-/blob/8d0a979cbb497e64ac6ebbf902beb7e3d9f894b1/Product%20Sales%20%26%20Inventory%20Performance%20Dashboard.png

This dashboard provides inventory managers, procurement heads, and commercial directors with full visibility into stock inflows and outflows, inventory utilization rates, profit contribution by device, and procurement payment distribution.

---

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Inventory & Material Flow
* **Total Material In:** 277K units received
* **Average Material In:** 277 units
* **Total Material Out:** 144K units dispatched
* **Average Material Out:** 144 units
* **Pending Material:** 133K units in backlog
* **Pending %:** 48% (0.48) backlog rate
* **Material Utilization %:** 52% (0.52)
* **Unique Products:** 5 core products

### Financial & Sales Performance
* **Total Sales Revenue:** 58M
* **Total Profit:** 13M
* **Profit Margin:** 23% (0.23)
* **Profit per Unit:** 92.21
* **Total Purchase Cost:** 312K
* **Units Sold:** 144K units

## 📊 Visual Breakdown & Insights

### 1. Profitability & Cost Breakdown by Product
* **Sum of Profit by Product:**
  * **Laptop:** 4.8M
  * **Smartphone:** 3.8M
  * **Tablet:** 2.4M
  * **Monitor:** 1.9M
  * **Headphones:** 0.4M
* **Average Cost Price by Product:** Laptop commands the highest unit cost at 588, followed by Smartphone (439), Tablet (282), Monitor (223), and Headphones (48).

### 2. Inventory Procurement & Order Fulfillment
* **Quantity Sold, Pending, and Purchased by Product:**
  * **Smartphone:** 30K Sold | 27K Pending | 57K Purchased
  * **Headphones:** 30K Sold | 25K Pending | 55K Purchased
  * **Tablet:** 30K Sold | 26K Pending | 55K Purchased
  * **Monitor:** 30K Sold | 28K Pending | 58K Purchased
  * **Laptop:** 26K Sold | 27K Pending | 53K Purchased
* **Pending % by Product:** Backlog is evenly distributed across lines: Monitor (21%), Laptop (20%), Smartphone (20%), Tablet (19%), and Headphones (19%).

### 3. Category & Procurement Channel Share
* **Quantity % Sold by Category:** Electronics dominates volume with 59%, followed by Accessories (21%) and Peripherals (20%).
* **Purchase Cost % by Payment Method:** Balanced procurement spend across channels: Bank Transfer (28%), Cash (26%), Credit Card (25%), and PayPal (22%).

## 🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Filters

* **Date of Sale:** Dynamic timeline filtering.
* **Category:** Slice performance across Electronics, Accessories, and Peripherals.
* **Payment Method:** Deep dive into specific settlement modes.
* **Product Name:** Granular single/multi-product filtering.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** Power Query ( material flow calculations, inventory modeling)
* **Calculations:** DAX (Measures for Material Utilization %, Unit Profit, Pending Backlog %, and Margin %)
* **UI/UX Design:** Dark neon purple/gradient theme with KPI card columns and segmented donut visuals
